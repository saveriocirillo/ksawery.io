*October 17, 2026*

I missed Las Wolski in the morning, with its October colors.

![A dirt trail curving through a beech forest, with sunlight cutting through the morning mist and fallen leaves on the ground](../../../assets/trails/las-wolski-trail.jpg)

Climbing a little to watch the fog lift, the sound of my steps in the leaves, a few scattered thoughts along the way.

![The view from a high point over Las Wolski: green treetops, a layer of fog in the valley, and distant hills under a clear blue sky](../../../assets/trails/las-wolski-fog.jpg)

I hope to give more time to trail running again. Let's go!
